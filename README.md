# 🍹 JuiceMakerApp

## Overview

JuiceMakerApp is a simple yet powerful application that allows users to create their own juice recipes with precise ingredient quantities. Just like cooking a meal with the right proportions, JuiceMakerApp helps you craft the perfect juice blend by specifying exactly what goes into it.

## Features

- **Custom Juice Creation**: Name and create your own unique juice recipes
- **Precise Measurements**: Specify exact quantities and units for each ingredient
- **Recipe Storage**: Save your favorite juice recipes for future reference
- **User-Friendly Interface**: Simple step-by-step process for creating juices
- **Ingredient Management**: Easily add or remove ingredients before finalizing your juice

## How It Works

1. **Name Your Juice**: Start by giving your juice creation a name
2. **Add Ingredients**: Specify each ingredient with name, quantity, and unit
3. **Make Your Juice**: Generate your custom juice recipe
4. **Save & Share**: Keep your recipes and share them with others
